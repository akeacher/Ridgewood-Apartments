Out The Front Door

Green in every direction

Ridgewood sits on the eastern edge of Saint Paul, where the street grid starts giving way to parkland. Two buildings around an oak courtyard, and minutes from the hiking and biking trails at Battle Creek and Pig’s Eye Lake.

On foot

Conway Park

Ball fields, a playground, and the rec centre — a half mile up the street.

5 min

Battle Creek Regional Park

Hiking and mountain-bike trails through the woods, plus a ski hill, the waterfall, and a dog park.

Minutes

Pig’s Eye Lake

Hiking and biking trails around the lake and out through the Mississippi bottoms.

4 min

Pig’s Eye Heron Rookery

Great blue herons and egrets nesting on the island in the Mississippi — one of the largest colonies in the state.

5 min

Indian Mounds Regional Park

Bluff trails along the top of the Mississippi river valley.

6 min

Beaver Lake

A fishing lake with a path around the shoreline.

Lake Phalen

A swimming beach, boat rental, golf, and a loop trail around the water.

8 min

Bruce Vento Nature Sanctuary

Restored prairie and wetland tucked in below the bluffs.

Conway Park is walkable — a half mile up the street, with the rec centre and a 24-hour gym at the end of the block. Everything else here is an easy ride. The hiking and biking trails at Battle Creek and Pig’s Eye Lake are the two big ones — wooded singletrack on one side of the neighbourhood, river bottoms on the other — with the bluff paths at Indian Mounds, three lakes in between, and the heron rookery four minutes down the road. Times shown are by car, so allow a little longer on two wheels.
